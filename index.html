Why Chase?

More Than Just Climbing

Climbing is more than just pulling on rock. I help you integrate nutrition, gym training, and recovery strategies into your climbing journey so you see consistent progress — on and off the wall.

When you climb with me, you’re not just signing up for a one-and-done day on the rock — you’re stepping into a long-term partnership built on progression, safety and a whole lot of stoke.

🧠 Science-Backed, Experience-Driven

With a strong background in exercise science, I bring a deep understanding of biomechanics and movement efficiency to every session. That means I don’t just show you what to do — I help you understand why it works, so you can climb smarter, not harder.

👨‍🏫 A Teacher at Heart

As an experienced educator, I know how to break down complex skills into simple, actionable steps. Everyone learns differently, and I tailor my coaching so you can absorb it quickly and confidently — whether it’s your first time tying in or your hundredth pitch.
