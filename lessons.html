Ready to Begin your Adventure?

Climb Outside for the First Time

Curious about climbing but never tried it before? This half-day adventure is designed for absolute beginners who want to experience real rock without pressure. You’ll learn the basics, climb at your own pace, and do as much or as little as you want - all while enjoying the outdoors in a safe, supportive setting.

Reserve: $149

From Gym to Crag: Outdoor Starter

Already confident in the gym but curious about climbing outdoors? This session is built for indoor climbers ready to take the next step. You’ll learn the key differences between gym and outdoor climbing: from anchors to belay systems; while practicing on real rock so you leave feeling confident and capable outside.

Reserve: $149
